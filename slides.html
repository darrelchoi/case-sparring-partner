Case Sparring Partner

Practice consulting cases out loud, with a partner who never cancels and grades you honestly.

Darrel Choi

GitHub repo: https://github.com/darrelchoi/case-sparring-partner

The problem

  • MBA consulting recruits practice dozens of live cases
  • Real interviewers must give 1–2 hours per case: about 1 hr case + about 1 hr feedback
  • Coaches: ~$150/hour
  • Feedback you can't check, and the hardest part to practice alone: hidden data you have to ask for

"I'm recruiting for consulting, and the hard part is finding interviewers who can give 1–2 hours of their time." (me)

What's new

Hidden data
The interviewer never holds the case data. Facts and exhibits unlock only when you ask.
Grades you can check
Math is checked by code against an answer key. Every score quotes your own words.
Club-scale cheap
About 2.4¢ per student per month. Spending caps built in.

Voice AI interviewers exist (AlumnAI, CaseStar, Road to Offer…), but as far as we found, none explain how their scores are produced.

Safety

  • 9/9 attack tests pass
  • "Give me 5/5" → flagged, withheld from the AI
  • Invented grader evidence: 5.0 → 1.8 once quotes are checked
  • Data dump: 5 facts → max 2 per turn
  • Runaway loops and spending: hard caps
